Changelog
=========

.. release:: unreleased

  - :feature:`-` A zone's sunlit walls (the lagged ``w`` term) now include walls with windows: each outside wall counts by its solid area (less its windows and doors) as a share of the room's solid outside wall, times the sun on it. Before, only walls without windows counted, each in full. A pinned ``w`` is on the old scale; leave it empty to have it learned again.
  - :feature:`-` A warning in the log names a zone's area's temperature sensors when it has several and none is chosen in the area's settings (the zone then uses its thermostat's reading). Needs House Structure's ``temperature_choices``.
  - :feature:`-` Breaking: Home Thermal Lab needs `House Structure <https://github.com/minchinweb/ha-house-structure>`_, and each zone and contribution chart is a room (area) there. The sun comes only from the room's windows, roofs and windowless walls, at the bearings House Structure measures, and whether the room is below ground from the room. The house rotation, its roofs and shading on the integration's settings (and the OpenStreetMap drawing on that form), and the window faces, roofs, sunlit walls, shading and below-ground setting typed on zones and contributions, are removed; upgrading removes them from the saved settings and logs a warning naming each. Check that House Structure's rotation and roofs match before upgrading. A zone or contribution with no room in House Structure is skipped, with an error in the log. YAML zones and contributions need an ``area_id``. A thermostat or room sensor picked on a zone still wins over the room's.
  - :feature:`-` The sidebar page is now House Structure's **Thermal Lab** page, and the zone view is its **Zones** tab. The lab's own sidebar panel and its copy of Chart.js are gone (House Structure ships Chart.js). Without House Structure the sensors still work, but there is no page.
  - :feature:`-` **Heat flow** on House Structure's House tab: the watts across each inside wall and floor, out through each outside face, from the furnace and from the sun, now, from the fitted zone models (``heat_flow.py``).
  - :feature:`-` Each zone's fitted cooling rate is published for `Manual J <https://github.com/minchinweb/ha-manual-j>`_ (``hass.data["home_thermal_lab_fitted"]``), which fills its "UA fitted" column from it.
  - :feature:`-` Breaking: the Loads tab and the Manual J, S and D code moved to the Manual J integration. The envelope defaults (air leakage and R-values), the furnace and air conditioner nameplate on the energy sensor (AFUE, blower curve, sensible capacity, tons, the furnace's room) and the building sub-entry are removed; they are House Structure's Furnace, Air conditioner and Building parts and Manual J's settings now. Upgrading removes them from the saved settings and logs a warning naming each, so type them into House Structure and Manual J first (the lab's diagnostics from before the upgrade keep a copy). The energy sensors keep their per-state powers.
  - :support:`-` The install button and steps use Home Assistant 2026.11's **Marketplace**, which replaced HACS; HACS (before 2026.11) and copying by hand are still described.
  - :feature:`-` Loads tab: **doors** to outside from House Structure are their own surfaces in the Manual J, at a new **outside door R-value** setting (R-3 by default), and are taken out of their wall's area. A door with no size stays in the wall, and the room says so. Doors in internal walls are not losses, as internal walls are not.
  - :feature:`-` ``scripts/door_state.py`` (hypothesis 09) reads the door and the rooms on each side from House Structure's door sensors; ``--door`` picks another contact and ``--list-doors`` lists them.
  - :bug:`-` Only one zone had a forecast: zones are added one by one at startup, and a zone added while the house forecast was being worked out made it fail (``dictionary changed size during iteration``), leaving the other zones' forecast sensors unavailable. The forecast and the learning pass now work from a snapshot of the zones.
  - :bug:`-` Zone tab: the heating (and cooling) band showed no calls from other zones unless the model had fitted them as neighbours; it now shows every other thermostat's calls.
  - :bug:`-` Loads tab: the "Calculating" spinner stayed after the data loaded (its display rule beat ``hidden``).
  - :feature:`-` Loads tab: the furnace's design, actual and allowed sizes show BTU/h beside watts, as the air conditioner's show tons.
  - :bug:`-` Loads tab: a wall with no area in House Structure was dropped from the heat loss without a word, and a roof over several rooms was counted in full in each of them. A shared roof is now split between its rooms by floor area, and walls are sized as below.
  - :feature:`-` Loads tab: missing sizes are **assumed and flagged**. A room with a width and depth (or one wall's length along each axis) and a height is taken as a rectangle for its floor area and volume; a wall with no area takes its length, its axis of the room, or the side of a square room of the floor area, times its height or the room's. Each room shows an "assumed sizes" pill with a list of what was assumed under the table. A wall that cannot be sized, or with more window than wall, is named in the notes.
  - :feature:`-` Loads tab: a **basement floor** loses heat to the ground (an effective R-11 by default), and the envelope defaults (**air leakage, wall, roof and basement floor R-values**) can be set on the integration's Reconfigure form; the notes say which are still the built-in ones. A basement's below-grade depth may come from House Structure's house-wide default, which is noted on the room.
  - :feature:`-` Panel charts: the room chart and the outdoor chart are drawn one above the other on the same time axis, no longer on two y-axes of one chart. The room chart uses one fixed scale for every zone (from all zones' room temperatures and setpoints over the recorder's history, and their target bands). It shows the outdoor temperature only where it comes into that range, and the other rooms on the same thermostat (from House Structure) as faint lines. The outdoor chart keeps one scale all year, from the outdoor sensor's long-term statistics.
  - :feature:`-` Zones, contribution charts and heating-call statistics can take a room (area) from `House Structure <https://github.com/minchinweb/ha-house-structure>`_ (a new, separate integration describing the house's rooms, walls, roofs, windows and registers as devices in its areas): the thermostat from the room's register, the room sensor from the area, the sun from its windows, roofs and windowless walls at their own bearings and tilts, and whether it is below ground. The sensors reload when the rooms change; typed settings still work without it.
  - :feature:`-` The forecast sensors run all zones together, each fed its neighbours' forecast, and forecast the hybrid: the usual day (7-day profile with today's gap fading) plus the model's departure for today's weather and setpoints. A zone with no usable model gets the usual day. Zones are fitted with their neighbours (``n``, ``x``) when that is physical and forecasts better; ``neighbours`` on a zone chooses them, else the zone that heats most is the hub. The shadow controller uses the neighbours' forecast for a coupled model. The furnace's delivery lag (``furnace_lag``) is learned and simulated. The panel's model card shows the neighbour terms, the lag and the forecast method.
  - :feature:`-` Zone model neighbour terms: ``n`` (flow from the neighbouring zones' temperature) and ``x`` (warming during their furnace calls), learned and refined like the others; an interior zone may fit ``a`` as 0. ``shadow.simulate_house`` simulates zones together, each with its thermostat. Backtested in ``scripts/predict_rc.py`` (hypothesis 16).
  - :feature:`-` Thermal Lab panel in the sidebar: per-zone tiles, a chart of room, outdoor, setpoint and target band with the next day's forecast and the forecasts made 3 hours earlier, heating calls and forecast duty, and a model card with its grade and live scores. Chart.js is vendored and pinned by hash (``invoke vendor``), so the panel loads nothing from the internet. A dashboard view for apexcharts-card is in ``homeassistant/thermal_lab_dashboard.yaml``.
  - :feature:`-` Each shadow zone also gets a forecast sensor (the room 1, 3 and 6 hours ahead from the zone model with a simulated thermostat following yesterday's program; its history is the archive of forecasts as issued, scored against the room every 6 hours) and a target sensor (a configurable target wave, or the setpoint). The outdoor forecast is corrected by its bias against the outdoor sensor.
  - :feature:`-` Zone model fitting after ClimateAdvisor: rates over hour-long blocks, negative sun, wall and ground terms dropped, unphysical fits refused (``problem``), refinement on 3 hour simulated paths, the heating rate ``h`` and the sunlit-wall lag learned from history, and a ``grade`` from the model's error against holding the temperature. Exact exponential steps in the simulation.
  - :feature:`-` Backtest of the zone model with a simulated thermostat (``scripts/predict_rc.py``, hypothesis 16 revised).
  - :feature:`-` Shadow zones tell window faces (``windows``, was ``walls``) from sunlit solid walls (``sunlit_walls``, own lagged gain ``w``), and can be marked ``below_ground`` with an optional ``ground_entity``, adding a ground-coupling term ``b``; with no sensor the ground follows an annual wave (mean, swing and coldest day set on the integration) instead of the air, since basement soil lags the air by about 70 days. The contribution chart gains a ``walls`` band for sunlit walls. Breaking: ``walls`` is now ``windows``.
  - :feature:`-` Web setup: the sensor buttons are numbered in the order to add them; furnace powers can be given in BTU/h and cooling in tons; a furnace energy sensor learns the blower powers from a whole-house power sensor; a shadow zone learns its no-heat model from the recorder when ``a`` and ``c`` are left out; roof pitches can be a rise:run ratio such as ``4:12``; each form suggests a name; house rotation and roofs are set once on the integration, and temperature and power pickers show only matching sensors; roofs are named, so a room keeps its link when a pitch is edited or the roof renamed, and two roofs may share a face; the house rotation is measured from the OpenStreetMap building outline and drawn on the setup form.
  - :feature:`-` Set up from the web interface: the integration holds the outdoor sensor, and each sensor (shadow zone, heating-call statistics, furnace energy, contribution chart) is added as a sub-entry. YAML still works. Needs Home Assistant 2025.3.
  - :feature:`-` Renamed: repository ``ha-home-thermal-lab``, integration domain ``home_thermal_lab`` (was ``house_thermal_lab``); household names replaced by room labels, with real entity ids in ``.env`` (``HA_ENTITY_ALIASES``).
