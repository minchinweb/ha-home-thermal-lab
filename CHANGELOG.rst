Changelog
=========

.. release:: unreleased

  - :feature:`-` New standalone integration, House Structure (``house_structure``): the house's rooms, outside walls, roofs, windows, internal walls and heating registers, each a device in a Home Assistant area. Sensors: sun on each wall and roof (W/m2, Open-Meteo every 15 minutes), solar gain through each window and each room's windows (W, less what a blind keeps out, following the cover's position), temperature difference across internal walls (from the areas' temperature sensors), and whether each register is heating. Sizes are typed in the house's unit and kept in metres.
  - :feature:`-` The forecast sensors run all zones together, each fed its neighbours' forecast, and forecast the hybrid: the usual day (7-day profile with today's gap fading) plus the model's departure for today's weather and setpoints. A zone with no usable model gets the usual day. Zones are fitted with their neighbours (``n``, ``x``) when that is physical and forecasts better; ``neighbours`` on a zone chooses them, else the zone that heats most is the hub. The shadow controller uses the neighbours' forecast for a coupled model. The furnace's delivery lag (``furnace_lag``) is learned and simulated. The panel's model card shows the neighbour terms, the lag and the forecast method.
  - :feature:`-` Zone model neighbour terms: ``n`` (flow from the neighbouring zones' temperature) and ``x`` (warming during their furnace calls), learned and refined like the others; an interior zone may fit ``a`` as 0. ``shadow.simulate_house`` simulates zones together, each with its thermostat. Backtested in ``scripts/predict_rc.py`` (hypothesis 16).
  - :feature:`-` Thermal Lab panel in the sidebar: per-zone tiles, a chart of room, outdoor, setpoint and target band with the next day's forecast and the forecasts made 3 hours earlier, heating calls and forecast duty, and a model card with its grade and live scores. Chart.js is vendored and pinned by hash (``invoke vendor``), so the panel loads nothing from the internet. A dashboard view for apexcharts-card is in ``homeassistant/thermal_lab_dashboard.yaml``.
  - :feature:`-` Each shadow zone also gets a forecast sensor (the room 1, 3 and 6 hours ahead from the zone model with a simulated thermostat following yesterday's program; its history is the archive of forecasts as issued, scored against the room every 6 hours) and a target sensor (a configurable target wave, or the setpoint). The outdoor forecast is corrected by its bias against the outdoor sensor.
  - :feature:`-` Zone model fitting after ClimateAdvisor: rates over hour-long blocks, negative sun, wall and ground terms dropped, unphysical fits refused (``problem``), refinement on 3 hour simulated paths, the heating rate ``h`` and the sunlit-wall lag learned from history, and a ``grade`` from the model's error against holding the temperature. Exact exponential steps in the simulation.
  - :feature:`-` Backtest of the zone model with a simulated thermostat (``scripts/predict_rc.py``, hypothesis 16 revised).
  - :feature:`-` Shadow zones tell window faces (``windows``, was ``walls``) from sunlit solid walls (``sunlit_walls``, own lagged gain ``w``), and can be marked ``below_ground`` with an optional ``ground_entity``, adding a ground-coupling term ``b``; with no sensor the ground follows an annual wave (mean, swing and coldest day set on the integration) instead of the air, since basement soil lags the air by about 70 days. The contribution chart gains a ``walls`` band for sunlit walls. Breaking: ``walls`` is now ``windows``.
  - :feature:`-` Web setup: the sensor buttons are numbered in the order to add them; furnace powers can be given in BTU/h and cooling in tons; a furnace energy sensor learns the blower powers from a whole-house power sensor; a shadow zone learns its no-heat model from the recorder when ``a`` and ``c`` are left out; roof pitches can be a rise:run ratio such as ``4:12``; each form suggests a name; house rotation and roofs are set once on the integration, and temperature and power pickers show only matching sensors; roofs are named, so a room keeps its link when a pitch is edited or the roof renamed, and two roofs may share a face; the house rotation is measured from the OpenStreetMap building outline and drawn on the setup form.
  - :feature:`-` Set up from the web interface: the integration holds the outdoor sensor, and each sensor (shadow zone, heating-call statistics, furnace energy, contribution chart) is added as a sub-entry. YAML still works. Needs Home Assistant 2025.3.
  - :feature:`-` Renamed: repository ``ha-home-thermal-lab``, integration domain ``home_thermal_lab`` (was ``house_thermal_lab``); household names replaced by room labels, with real entity ids in ``.env`` (``HA_ENTITY_ALIASES``).
  - :feature:`-` No-fan nights experiment (Home Assistant automations, hypotheses 08 and 10), detected in the fan mixing script.
  - :feature:`-` Office lights on a dimmer as a modelled load (scripts and the contribution sensor's ``lights`` option); HACS "add repository" button in the README.
  - :feature:`-` Package the integration for HACS (``hacs.json``, manifest version and issue tracker, HACS and hassfest validation workflow); no ``async_setup`` needed.
  - :feature:`-` New sensors: heating-call statistics per zone, modelled furnace energy (blower and gas) for the energy dashboard, and per-zone contribution split for a stacked chart.
  - :feature:`-` Contribution split (history and forecast) and its fitting script; furnace energy meter; heating-call analysis (lag, rise, overshoot, dip, rebound).
  - :feature:`-` Fan mixing test and its review (hypothesis 08), door-edge study at 5-minute bins (09), room network fit (10), furnace lag (13), plug-load heat and blower power (14), plug terms in the occupancy fit (15), shower log from bathroom humidity (18).
  - :feature:`-` Tampa, Sherbrooke and Victoria (city, not the airport) as example locations; retry the Open-Meteo archive when it is overloaded.
